# Psych Alchemy — Practitioner Processes (app import)

Source: *Psych Alchemy · Practitioner Edition*, Part 3. Script lines are verbatim. Placeholders are in [square brackets].

## Standing pre-step — every process, every time

**Script:**

> "Close your eyes. Soften the back of your neck and let your shoulders drop. Let your jaw go — teeth apart, tongue soft. Let your eyes soften behind the lids, like they're resting back in your head. Now relax your brain, consciously — let it go soft and heavy. Good. Now bring your attention down into the body."

**Order:** **neck → jaw → eyes → relax the brain → attention to the trunk.** If the client can't get past the jaw, they are not ready for the belief yet — stay here, or go back to nervous system work.

**Self-screen:** Same sequence, silently, before you start. Thirty seconds.

**Wording:** Wording is standardised. The oscillation question in identity work is **"What are you when you are not being [identity]?"**

## 3.1 ALLOW — the base practice (self)

Not a clearing protocol. A way of meeting stored charge at the layer where it lives — pre-categorical felt sense, in the trunk — so it can move on its own. The reps build every practitioner meta-skill at once.

| | Step | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Attention before analysis | Notice what's here before naming it. Texture, location, temperature, movement — not "anger" or "grief" | Posterior insula (raw interoception) before anterior insula (labelling). Categories are always too small |
| L | Let go of tension | Release the cranial brakes in order: **neck/upper traps → jaw → eyes → relax your brain consciously** | CN XI → CN V → CN III/IV/VI, then a conscious softening of the brain itself before attention drops to the trunk. Caudal to rostral, walking the autonomic ladder into parasympathetic. Reversing the order fails |
| L | Lean into the felt sense | Turn attention to the dominant trunk region — throat, heart, gut, pelvis. Pattern A: open first, then meet. Pattern B: meet first, opening emerges | The constriction *is* the felt sense at this stage. Lean into the armour, not past it |
| O | Observe without changing | Stay. Don't fix, release, resolve or transmute | Doing-mode recreates threat physiology and closes the reconsolidation window. The felt sense moves when you stop trying to move it |
| W | Welcome the sensation | Host it. Say yes to what's here, including your resistance to it | Stance, not technique. Welcomed it relaxes; resisted it intensifies; suppressed it goes underground |

### Felt Sense Access Protocol (in order)

1. **Neck** — upper traps · CN XI
2. **Jaw** — masseter · CN V
3. **Eyes** — soften behind the lids · CN III/IV/VI
4. **Relax your brain consciously**
5. **Heart** — attention drops to the trunk
6. **Open or meet** — A: open first, then meet / B: meet first, opening emerges

**The three layers.** *Surface* — where sensation presents (chest tightness). *Avoidance reflex* — attention slides away as you approach the material; this is the diagnostic signal, not the obstacle. *Root* — where it actually lives, usually lower than the surface suggests (chest → diaphragm → pelvic floor). Integration happens at the root.

**Dominant region is diagnostic.** Throat: expression and voice. Heart: attachment and relational material. Gut: safety, boundary, disgust. Pelvis: core identity, sexuality, pre-verbal material.

**Integration is not clearing.** Met accurately, felt sense *moves* — toward resolution, toward deeper recognition, or toward correct relationship. Some material resolves in minutes; some over months; some (real grief, mortality) isn't supposed to resolve; some needs another regulated nervous system in the room.

**Delineation from the clearing processes.** ALLOW has no oscillation and no object beyond the felt sense. You don't reach for the preferred state; you let it arrive once the avoided state is fully met. The clearing processes add the steering wheel (belief / identity / frozen moment / goal) and the deliberate juxtaposition. But every clearing process *contains* ALLOW: steps 1–2 of the loop ("feel that — what does that feel like?") are A-L-L-O-W compressed into two questions, and the cranial brakes are released before any loop starts — see the standing pre-step at the top of Part 3.

**When to teach clients:** early, for most — it's how they navigate between sessions. Expect to teach it 6–12 times. **Exception:** highly traumatised or highly activated — not in the first 3–6 sessions; stabilise first. **Stop** if they flip into fight/flight/freeze, dissociate, or surface material they can't metabolise alone.

### Where it goes sideways (applies to every process)

*Doing-mode collapse* — using the practice to get rid of feelings faster; suppression with better marketing. *Spiritual bypass* — profound reports with no somatic detail; dissociation in spiritual costume. Test: can they describe exactly where, what texture, what temperature, how it changed? If not, something other than ALLOW is happening.

## 3.2 Blockage Shifting

### Entry

**Use when:** overwhelmed by many problems, confused about the core, mental loops, "too much".

**Opening:** "Are you having a hard time working out what the main issue is?" → "Out of everything, which one *seems like* the right one to start with?"

**Entry:** "If you were to name one thing that's a problem right now, what would it be?"

**Prime:** Three nasal breaths, long exhale; feet, seat, room. "Nothing needs fixing or forcing."

**If "I don't know / can't feel it" at C or D:** "Guess what it would feel like." If still nothing: "Feel that you don't know — what does that feel like?"

### Loop

*Repeat until: "it's gone" / "there's no problem" / "I can't find it" / no charge*

A. "Feel [problem] — what does it feel like?"
B. "Feel [last response] — what does that feel like?"
C. "Feel the problem you have right now — what would it feel like to *not* have this problem?"
D. "Feel [last response] — what does that feel like?"
E. "What's the problem now?"

### Checks

- "Will it come back?"
- "Any scenario?"
- "Anything else here?"
- If yes → describe the problem now.

### Return

Either loop again at A, or it's now a clean belief/identity → hand off to 3.3 / 3.4.

### Integrate

**Anchor:** "What's different now?" · "If this was a belief about yourself, what would it have been?" · "What's a more empowering belief that fits now?" **Future-pace:** "Imagine a situation that would have triggered this. How do you respond now?"

### Self-screen

"If I named one thing that's a problem right now, what would it be?" → Feel it — what does it feel like? → Feel that — what does that feel like? → What would it feel like to not have this problem? → Feel that — what does that feel like? → What's the problem now? Repeat until there's nothing to find. Then: "If this was a belief about myself, what would it have been?"

### Where it goes sideways

- **The problem keeps changing** — that's the process working; each new "problem" at E is a layer. Keep going until there's no charge, not until it makes sense.
- **They name a belief or identity at E** — stop looping; it's now a clean object. Hand off to 3.3 / 3.4.
- **They go into story** — bring them back to the body: "and what does that feel like?"
- **Nothing shifts after three rounds** — the system may not have permission or resources; check both, or go back to nervous system work.

## 3.3 Belief Shifting

### Entry

**Belief known:** client states it. Muscle test — locks = active.

**Belief unknown:** "Define the problem in a few words." → "Think of [problem]. What do you believe about yourself when you're experiencing that?" → test it.

**Detective sequence** if nothing surfaces: "If you weren't suffering with this, what would you be able to do?" → "How would that make you feel?" → "Why can't you feel that now?" (beliefs fall out here) → "If nothing changed, how would you feel?"

Extract statements **word for word**. When several branch from one, the root is the one the others logically depend on (worked example: I don't have a choice / I'm not allowed to be angry / people will take advantage → *I'm not safe*).

**Identity check (always, before the loop):** "What kind of person believes [belief]?" → answer = go to 3.4 first. Blank = continue.

**Permission:** "Is this a priority now? Do I have permission to correct this?" (test). No → what resource is needed / what clears first.

### Loop

*Eyes closed, answers brief, first thing that comes.*

1. "Feel yourself believing '[belief]' — what does it feel like?"
2. "Feel [response] — what does [response] feel like?"
3. "What would you rather feel?"
4. "What would [preferred] feel like?"
5. "Feel [response] — what does that feel like?"
6. "Do you still believe '[belief]'?"  
   → *Yes → 1 · No → checks*

### Checks

- "Does any part of you still believe it?" — *→ yes → loop*
- "Could you believe it again in the future?" — *→ yes → loop, oriented to future*
- "Is there any scenario in which you'd still believe it?" — *→ yes → loop inside that scenario*
- Re-test: "[negative belief]" unlocks · "[positive belief]" locks

### Return

If identity was cleared: identity → belief → original problem, each "still a problem?" → no.

### Integrate

"How do you feel now?" · "What are you more conscious of now?" → new narrative → one action, dated. Daily 5 min feeling into the new state; one irreversible action from it.

### Self-screen

State the belief; body-dowse or simply ask "does this feel true, even if I don't want it to?" → "Feel myself believing [belief] — what does it feel like?" → "Feel [that] — what does that feel like?" → "What would I rather feel?" → "What would [preferred] feel like?" → "Feel [that] — what does that feel like?" → "Do I still believe it? Any part of me? In the future? In any scenario?" Re-state the positive. Daily 5–10 minutes. Nick: "when I stopped doing it, my bank account goes down."

## 3.4 Identity Shifting

### Entry

**Entry doors:** a problem (pain, anxiety, gut), a trigger (person, situation), a pattern (people-pleasing, perfectionism, control, procrastination) — or a belief that answered "what kind of person believes that?"

Settle the body as you go — uncross arms, hands on thighs. When it intensifies (shaking, heat), hold them there within safety: "we're staying right here."

### Phase 1 — map

1. "What would you like to work on?"
2. "When you think of [that], what emotion do you feel in your body?"
3. "Where do you feel that in your body?"
4. "When you notice that sensation, how do you behave?" → **stuck identity** ("I become the one who takes care of everyone" → the Caretaker / "worthless person")

### Loop — Phase 2: collapse

1. "Feel yourself being [identity] — what does it feel like?"
2. "Feel [response] — what happens in yourself when you feel [response]?"
3. "What are you when you are *not* being [identity]?"  
   → *inverse identity*
4. "Feel yourself being [inverse] — what does that feel like?"
5. "Feel [response] — what happens in yourself when you feel that?"
6. "Can you still feel yourself being [identity]?"  
   → *Yes → 1 · No → Phase 3*

### Checks

- "Do you think you might feel yourself being [identity] in the future?" — *→ yes → Phase 2 in the future*
- "Is there any scenario in which you might still be [identity]?" — *→ yes → Phase 2 in that scenario*

### Phase 4 — reassess

 "Feel [problem/trigger/pattern] — does it still feel like a problem?" No → integrate.

### Return

Yes → another identity or a belief underneath → back to Phase 1.

### Integrate

Awareness questions (what are you more conscious of? new narrative? intention?) → action questions (what needs to happen? the one thing? first action? when?).

### Self-screen

"When I think of [problem/trigger/pattern], what emotion do I feel? Where? When I notice that, how do I behave?" → name the identity → "Feel myself being [identity] — what does it feel like? Feel [that] — what happens in me? What am I when I am not being [identity]? Feel myself being [inverse] — what does that feel like? Feel [that] — what happens in me? Can I still feel myself being [identity]?" → future / scenario → back to the entry: "does it still feel like a problem?"

### Where it goes sideways — troubleshooting

- **"I don't know who I am when I'm not that"** → feel the *absence*; "what quality wants to emerge when that's gone?" Let it arise; don't force a label.
- **Comes back** → hierarchy (another identity under it), context-dependence, or secondary gain ("what would you lose if you fully let this go?").
- **Shifts in session, not in life** → nervous system can't hold it under load; environment/relational field re-pulls. More E first; daily practice; neurological consolidation (reflex, brain zone, cranial, rocking, TRE).

## 3.5 Trauma Shifting

### Entry

**Use when:** a memory is replaying or repeatedly triggering, or you suspect it's running underneath a PNI/somatic response. Don't give a traumatic event extra weight unless it's clearly a primary driver.

**Is it active?** "When was the first time you felt this?" If it surfaces instantly, it's active and sitting in the limbic system — go there. If it doesn't, don't excavate.

**Combine with the Emotions Process** for dramas/emotional events (accident, loss, breakup) — still take it to the worst part if it's safe to go there.

**1. Permission + sovereignty:** "Do we have permission to work through this memory?" Confirm with the body (felt sense and/or muscle test). No → stop.

### Loop

*Eyes closed.*

2. "Bring your mind to the memory. Note the age. Take your mind to the *worst part* — the worst moment. Freeze it there. Keep feeling that frozen moment."  
   → *usually one snapshot: a vision, a feeling, a sensory imprint*
3. "In this frozen moment, what kind of person are you being?"  
   → *stuck identity*
4. Run the identity loop (3.4 Phase 2) on that identity, including future and scenario checks, until it's no longer accessible.

### Checks

5. "Take your mind back to the worst part. Does it still feel like a problem?" — *Yes → extract the next identity layer (one moment can hold 1–3). No → confirm.*
6. **Confirm neutrality.** Muscle test while they hold the moment — no inhibition, no tone change. Neutral = reconsolidated.

### Return

Afterwards re-check whether the surface beliefs are still active — often they've gone quiet on their own.

### Integrate

7. **Optional (Kali):** "Is there something your younger self wanted to hear, or be sent?" — a word, an emotion, a touch. Have them send it. Not a required step.
8. **Open eyes, integrate.** "What are you more conscious of now?"

### Self-screen

Only with a regulated system and only for memories you already know are active. Permission first — ask the body. Go to the worst frozen moment; "what kind of person am I being in this moment?" → run the identity self-screen on it → return to the moment: "still a problem?" → if not, close. If anything intensifies beyond what you can hold, stop and bring it to a practitioner — this is the one process where the self version has a hard ceiling.

### Where it goes sideways

- **They can't find the worst moment** — it may not be active; don't excavate. Go back to the entry question or work the pattern instead.
- **The moment won't freeze — it keeps playing** — have them pick one frame: "if it was a photograph, which one?"
- **The felt sense intensifies (shaking, heat, tears)** — hold them there within safety: "we're staying right here." Don't rescue; don't push.
- **They go blank or drift** — dorsal shutdown. Stop, orient to the room, regulate. This is a hard stop, not a technique problem.
- **Neutral in the room, charged next week** — another identity layer in the same moment, or a second moment. Re-test and extract again.

## 3.6 Identity Alignment

### Entry

**Not** affirmation, motivation or reframing. Updating the nervous system's predictive model of self so a desired reality is available to it.

**Goal / desired reality / state:** "$30k months by July", "fully booked", "confident raising rates", "powerful".

Or via the inverse test: positive statement muscle-inhibits → "What kind of person believes that state is possible?"

3. **Extract:** "Imagine you've already achieved that. What kind of person are you being?" → target identity ("a powerful person"). *If this is very hard, they're too limbic — go back to clearing or nervous system work.*

4. **Embody:** "Feel yourself being [target] — what does that feel like?" → "Feel [response] — what does that feel like?"

### Loop — surface and metabolise resistance

5. "Why would you *not* be [target]?"  
   → *reason ("I'm small", "it's too hard", "I'm afraid")*
6. "Feel [reason] — what does that feel like?"
7. "What would it feel like to not have that problem?"
8. "Feel [solution] — what does that feel like?"
9. "Do you feel like you are now [target]?"  
   → *No → 5 · Yes → checks*

### Checks

10. "Do you feel like you will be [target] in the future?" — *No → 5, oriented to future*
11. "Is there any scenario in which you would not be [target]?" — *Yes → 5, inside that scenario*
12. "Do you now know you can *maintain* being [target] to achieve that reality?" — *No → "Why not?" → 5*

### Return

"Does [goal] now feel inevitable?" No → extract the next identity (1–3 per goal).

### Integrate

**Completion markers:** slower breath, stable eye contact, reduced micro-tension, vocal steadiness, *absence of hype*. It should feel normal, grounded, inevitable — not exciting.

### Self-screen

"Imagine I've already achieved [goal]. What kind of person am I being?" → "Feel myself being [target] — what does it feel like? Feel [that] — what does that feel like?" → "Why would I not be [target]?" → "Feel [reason] — what does it feel like? What would it feel like to not have that problem? Feel [that]." → "Am I [target] now? Will I be in the future? Any scenario where I wouldn't be? Can I maintain it?" → "Does [goal] feel inevitable?" Check your own breath, jaw and voice — if you're hyped, you're performing it.

### When it will not work — do clearing / nervous system first

- **Limbic dominance** — future feels unsafe; extraction returns blank or distorted ("I'd be fake / arrogant / I'd lose people")
- **Freeze / dorsal shutdown** — "I don't know", nothing arises
- **Trauma-bound self-schema** — victim, rejection, scarcity, moral superiority around struggle
- **Cognitive compliance** — right answers, shallow breath, tight jaw, strained voice

### Final anchor

"Does [goal] now feel inevitable?" No → "Why not? Does another identity need to be built in?" → extract the next identity (typically 1–3 per goal).

### Clean rule

Alignment works when the system can simulate the future, access interoception accurately, tell fear from truth, and the identity feels like *remembering*, not forcing. Alignment is phase-two work.

## 3.7 Reality Shifting

*Wrapper flow — routes to the other processes; not a loop.*

Moving toward a goal, and clearing whatever the movement flushes up. The practitioner teaches it; the client (or practitioner as their own No.1 client) runs it.

### Flow

1. **Calibrate** a yes/no (body dowse: forward = yes, back = no; or muscle test).
2. **Set the goal** in writing.
3. **Identity statement test** "I am the version of me who [goal]."  
   → *Yes → already held; deepen embodiment · No → clear ↓*
4. **What's blocking — belief or identity?**
   - **Belief:** "What would I have to believe to *not* believe I'm this person?" **→ 3.3**
   - **Identity:** "What kind of person do I become when I don't believe I can achieve this?" **→ 3.4**
   - **Won't hold:** Positive state won't hold at all **→ 3.6**
   - In this edition all clearing routes through 3.3 / 3.4 / 3.6. The affirmation step used in the Integrated Healer Program is not part of the practitioner protocol — see Part 2 for why.
5. **Retest the identity statement**  
   → *Still no → repeat 4*
6. **Build the feeling and detach** — the state of having it, in the now; release when/how.
7. **SATs** (evening): a short scene implying the wish fulfilled, fall asleep in it.
8. **Irreversible action** from the new identity — change pricing, post publicly, say no to something, add a clinic day. Action locks identity.

### Where it goes sideways

- **The identity statement tests yes but nothing changes** — the goal is too small or too safe; the body already holds it. Pick a goal that produces a no.
- **Every clear reveals another block** — normal for a big goal; typically 1–3 identities per reality. Stop when the statement holds across future and scenario.
- **They skip to step 6 and build the feeling without clearing** — that's affirmation on a closed network (Part 2). Clear first.
- **The feeling is hype, not settledness** — sympathetic, not integrated. Slow down; check the completion markers in 3.6.
- **No irreversible action taken** — the shift won't hold. The action is the anchor, not an optional extra.

### Self-screen

This *is* the self version — the practitioner teaches it, the client (or you, as your own No. 1 client) runs it. Steps 1–5 above, with the belief/identity clear done via the self-screens in 3.3 / 3.4 / 3.6.
