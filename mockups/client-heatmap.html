Client heat map

Two things matter: how recently someone booked, and how much history they actually have with you. Each client gets a recency score (R) and a traction score (E) based on total sessions, added together for one priority score. Colour shows discipline, and the background deepens as the score rises.

Priority = Recency+Traction ·R6 booked → R0 over 90 days ·E5 15+ sessions → E1 one session

Where to focus

Recency and traction crossed — this is the view that actually tells you who to chase.

All clients

Group tiles by priority, discipline or recency. Within each group the strongest clients come first.

Recency uses the latest non-cancelled appointment or non-cancelled/non-rescheduled voice booking on or before today; a future booking overrides it. Traction counts all sessions on record, including old ones — so a long-standing client who has slipped gets a higher priority than a one-off who booked recently. “$0” marks a free client. K = kinesiology, V = voice, P = piano.
